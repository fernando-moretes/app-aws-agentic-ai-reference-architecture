# Security Policy

This is a public portfolio and reference-architecture repository. Do not commit secrets, real customer data, production account identifiers or internal banking information.

## Reporting

For security issues, open a private advisory or contact the repository owner through the public profile links.

## Secure design principles

- Least privilege by default.
- No secrets in source control.
- Explicit threat modeling for new architecture patterns.
- Human approval for high-risk AI or financial operations.
